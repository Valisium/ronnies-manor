    How to play

    Swipe a tile, or tap one tile then the one next to it, to swap them. Line up three or more the same to clear them.

    • 4 in a line: Black Cab. Clears a whole row or column.
    • L or T shape: Big Ben. Blasts the tiles around it.
    • 5 in a line: Pearly Button. Swap it with a tile to clear every tile of that colour.
    • 2×2 square: The Wood. Bowls across the board on a curve.
    • Tap a power-up twice to set it off where it is. Swap two power-ups together for a big combo.
